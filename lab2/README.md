## 3. Sprint Simulation Reflection Summary

1. **Effort vs. Estimation:** Low-complexity stories (e.g., Jira time logging at 3 points, ticket validation at 2 points) were delivered predictably. Higher-complexity tasks (budget burn rate visualization at 8 points) correctly reflected high architectural complexity and remained open at sprint close due to capacity limits.
2. **Backlog Prioritization:** Aligned strictly with primary user flows (logging hours, timesheet submission, approvals, budget tracking). Breaking down large epics into smaller incremental stories would improve velocity consistency.
3. **Sprint Alignment:** Demonstrated the impact of carry-over work across sprints (13-point mid-sprint scope addition), reinforcing the need for conservative velocity planning.
4. **Burndown Insights:** Identified sprint bottlenecks and recommended decomposing complex 8-point stories into modular sub-tasks to ensure steady burn-down.
